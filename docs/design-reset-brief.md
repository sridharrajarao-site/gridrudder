# GridRudder website design reset brief

## Why the current direction is being reset

Two iterations changed color balance without solving the founder's lack of conviction. The problem is therefore not a single shade or section. The next design must reconsider hierarchy, composition, typography, density, product visualization, proof placement, and brand character together.

## Audience

Primary: GPU infrastructure operators, data-center and colocation teams, platform engineering leaders, and technical design partners.

Secondary: investors, energy/facility partners, and engineers assessing the open-source agent.

## Product truth

- GridRudder is supervised software that translates power constraints into bounded GPU actions.
- Operators approve actions; the current product is not autonomous production control.
- Retained evidence supports one reversible single-host observation: GPU 156.22→124.37 W and BMC server 291→260 W.
- Application throughput, fleet behavior, generalized savings, and production readiness are not proven.
- Simulator and safety software have 203 passing tests; this is software evidence, not physical-performance evidence.
- The initial offer is a supervised design-partner pilot.

## Design requirements

1. A qualified buyer can state what the product does, for whom, and why it matters within 30 seconds.
2. The visual language reads as calm, technical, precise, and operational—not crypto, generic AI, environmental marketing, or consumer SaaS.
3. Physical evidence and its limitation are inseparable in the hierarchy.
4. The grid → policy → approval → GPU → independent verification loop is understandable without reading a wall of copy.
5. One primary pilot action is visually obvious, with email as a secondary path.
6. Color is semantic and restrained; brand recognition cannot depend on a large saturated surface.
7. Desktop and mobile compositions are intentionally designed, not merely stacked.
8. Accessibility, fast rendering, and honest copy are release gates.

## Committee scoring

Each direction receives 0–10 scores for:

- enterprise trust;
- immediate comprehension;
- distinctiveness;
- evidence credibility;
- conversion clarity;
- accessibility and mobile quality;
- implementation durability.

Any direction scoring below 8 in enterprise trust, comprehension, or evidence credibility is rejected regardless of average. No production deployment occurs until the Chief Architect selects a direction after cross-review.
