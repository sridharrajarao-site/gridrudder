# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in GridRudder to be safe, constructive, and accessible. Be respectful, assume good faith while still examining evidence, welcome questions, give actionable technical feedback, and protect confidential or personal information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, deliberate humiliation, publishing another person’s private information, or encouraging unsafe or unauthorized testing.

## Safety-sensitive collaboration

Disagreement about architecture, evidence, or safety is expected. Describe risks concretely, separate measurements from inference, disclose uncertainty, and stop a hardware experiment when an authorized operator or documented guardrail requires it. Never pressure someone to reveal credentials or bypass an organization’s controls.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that violates this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, recurrence, and the safety or privacy of affected people.

Email `pilot@gridrudder.com` with a subject beginning `[CONDUCT]` to report a concern privately. Include only the information needed to understand the report; ask for an access-controlled transfer method for sensitive attachments. Reports should not be filed publicly when doing so would expose personal or security-sensitive information.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.
