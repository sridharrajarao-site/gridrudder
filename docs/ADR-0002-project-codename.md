# ADR-0002: Project codename is LoadHelm

Status: Accepted by Chief Architect, 2026-08-30

## Decision

The internal codename is **Project LoadHelm**. Architecture diagrams and pilot drafts may use **LoadHelm Control Plane** with the descriptor **grid-aware power control for AI clusters**.

Working tagline:

> Hold the power envelope. Protect the workload.

The codename accurately joins utility load and compute load, communicates supervised direction, and remains extensible across accelerators, schedulers, batteries, cooling, and multiple sites. It must not be described as autonomous grid control, protective equipment, or a replacement for utility/facility safety systems.

This decision does not authorize a company name, product launch, domain purchase, or trademark filing. Formal legal, linguistic, and jurisdictional clearance is required first. Repository and Python package names remain unchanged until an external pilot establishes the public product boundary.

See `naming-recommendations.md` for the council scorecard and collision screen.
